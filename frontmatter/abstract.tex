This document is one possible \LaTeX\ framework for a thesis or dissertation at the University of Victoria. It should compile in all common environments, and its content is based on the experience of one student (Samuel Fielder; hi, that's me!) and his supervisory committee. It shows the structure that the University expects of a thesis, and helps with some of the formatting problems that come up in astronomy and physics, such as long tables and complex figures. Dozens of students have used this template over the past years, with minimal formatting changes needed for submission to UVicSpace.

In the fall of 2026, the template was rewritten around a single document class. This greatly simplifies the project: instead of editing many individual setup macros, students now choose a few class options and fill in their thesis details in one place. Students who used the previous version of the template for their MSc and are returning to it for their PhD dissertation need to make very few changes to their existing chapters: set up a copy of this template as described in this document, then bring in their own content.
